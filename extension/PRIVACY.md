# Web Detox: privacy policy

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

Web Detox is made by Sindre Sørensen. It is designed to collect nothing about you.

## What Web Detox does not do

- It has no account, no sign-in and no servers of its own.
- It collects no personal data, no usage statistics and no analytics, and it contains no tracking or advertising code.
- It does not sell, share or send any data to the developer or to third parties.

## What stays on your device

- **Settings:** your reading levels, the topics you have switched off, section choices and sort order are saved in your browser's extension storage on your own device.
- **Saved and read articles:** the articles you put in Read later or Bookmarks (address, headline, lead, picture address and site), and which articles you have opened in plain view (for "Hide read", kept 30 days), in the same local storage.
- **News sites you visit:** for My news, which news sites you have visited and how often lately (a count per site that fades over two weeks, at most 60 sites), in the same local storage. It is not synced and never sent anywhere.
- **Article cache:** to show an article's own headline and lead, Web Detox stores the headline, lead, share image address, section and keywords of articles it has looked up, for up to 12 hours, in the same local storage. It is never sent anywhere.

## What goes to your other devices

So that Read later, Bookmarks, "Hide read" and your section choices are the same on all your devices, Web Detox keeps one small copy of them in your own sync storage: in Chrome, Edge and Firefox the browser's account sync (your Google, Microsoft or Firefox account), and in Safari your iCloud account, through the Web Detox app. The copy holds the address, headline and site of each saved article, the section layout of each site and the two list settings, and, for articles you have read in the last 14 days, a short code made from each address (not the address itself). It goes only to your own account at Google, Microsoft, Mozilla or Apple, under their privacy policies; the developer never sees it. If you don't use your browser's sync or iCloud, it stays on your device.

## Deleting your data

In the Web Detox popup, under "Your data", **Delete everything Web Detox has stored** deletes your settings, saved articles, reading history and article cache on this device at once, and the synced copy. Your other devices delete what they have of it the next time they sync. You can also remove the extension, or clear its data in your browser's settings.

## Which requests Web Detox makes

To find an article's real headline and lead, Web Detox loads the article pages and RSS feeds of the news sites you visit, directly from your device to those sites, the same way your browser would if you opened them. When you open an article in plain view, Web Detox loads that article the same way. My news loads the front pages of the news sites you visit most, and their articles, the same way. Those requests go only to the sites whose links are on the page you are reading. The news sites receive them as ordinary visits and handle them under their own privacy policies.

Web Detox needs permission to read the websites you visit for these reasons only: to find news teasers on the page and replace or rearrange them on your screen, and on social media sites to hide ads, suggestions and pop-ups on your screen. On those sites it makes no requests of its own and reads nothing beyond the page in front of you.

## Apple, TestFlight and the browser stores

If you install Web Detox through TestFlight or the App Store, Apple may collect crash reports and the feedback you choose to send, under Apple's own privacy policy. The developer can see what Apple shares from TestFlight (crash reports and feedback), and nothing else. The same goes for the Chrome Web Store, Microsoft Edge Add-ons and Firefox Add-ons.

## Kort på norsk

Web Detox samler ingen data om deg. Det finnes ingen konto, ingen egne servere, ingen sporing og ingen statistikk. Innstillingene dine, sakene du har lagret, hvilke saker du har lest og et midlertidig lager av overskrifter og ingresser (inntil 12 timer) ligger i nettleseren på din egen enhet. Lagrede saker, lesestatus (som korte koder, ikke adresser) og seksjonsoppsett synkroniseres til de andre enhetene dine via din egen nettleserkonto eller iCloud, aldri til oss. Mine nyheter husker hvilke nyhetsnettsteder du besøker og hvor ofte, bare på enheten din, og henter forsidene deres direkte fra nettstedene. Under «Dine data» i Web Detox-menyen kan du slette alt, også den synkroniserte kopien. For å finne artiklenes egne overskrifter henter Web Detox artikkelsider og RSS-feeder direkte fra nyhetsnettstedene du besøker, slik nettleseren ellers ville gjort. På sosiale medier skjuler Web Detox reklame, forslag og oppsprett på skjermen din, uten egne forespørsler. Installerer du via TestFlight eller App Store, kan Apple samle krasjrapporter og tilbakemeldinger du selv sender, etter Apples egne regler. Spørsmål: sindre@sedb.no
