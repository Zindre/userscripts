Read later and Bookmarks: save an article from the reader (the clock and bookmark buttons), with L or B on a keyboard, or by holding your finger on it in the list. Both show at the top of every site's list, with articles from all sites. Read later empties itself as you read.

My news: open the Web Detox menu and tap My news. It gathers the news sites you visit most and shows their front pages together, one section per site, or all of them with the most important first.

Your things on all your devices: Read later, Bookmarks, what you have read and your section choices follow you through iCloud. Under Your data, at the bottom of the menu, you can delete everything Web Detox has stored.

The list: hide what you have read (the switch above the sections). Edit the sections right in the row of buttons: tap the pencil, then tap a section to hide or show it, or drag it to move it. The section you are reading is marked. Leads keep the same size, so the list no longer jumps while it loads, and pictures no longer blink. On a wide screen, small sections stand side by side.

Web Detox now speaks 14 languages. The list follows the site's language; the menu and the app follow your device.

Also new: the app's first screen shows whether Web Detox is turned on and allowed on all websites, with a button to the right page in Settings. Safari's "get our app" banner is gone from news sites. "Read on the site" opens in a new tab, so you come straight back. Fixed: bild.de reloading over and over, and dark-mode extensions such as Noir changing the list's colours.
