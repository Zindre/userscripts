Snapchat for Web no longer offers to turn on notifications and hides Team Snapchat's chat; you can still turn notifications on in its settings. Calm mode also removes the "Open app" bar at the bottom of Facebook and Safari's "Open in the app" banner at the top of social media sites, and Snapchat for Web opens on a phone instead of a page telling you to use a computer. Turn Calm mode off for a site by picking Vanilla in the NoThis menu.
