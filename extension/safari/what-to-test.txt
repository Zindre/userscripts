Calm mode for social media: on Facebook, Instagram, X, Reddit, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok and 14 other sites, NoThis hides ads, suggested posts and accounts, Reels and Shorts, and pop-ups that push the app or a login. Sites can no longer open their app by themselves; you choose. Turn it off for a site by picking Vanilla in the NoThis menu. Also new: in Plain view, tapping an article right after a front page opens responds sooner, and headlines on ba.no no longer show stray code or doubled text.
