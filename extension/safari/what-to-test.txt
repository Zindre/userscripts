The app has a new icon: a window with a leaf. VG TV: a video page now shows just the video, its title and its text. The menu, the app banner and the lists of other videos are gone, though a series keeps its list of episodes. The ads in front of a video are skipped, which may also work on Aftenposten, Aftonbladet and other sites that use the same video player. If an ad still plays, check that Web Detox is allowed on the site in Safari's settings.
