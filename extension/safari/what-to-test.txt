On a phone, Snapchat's front page now goes straight to Snapchat for Web, which should open there instead of a page telling you to use a computer. It no longer offers to turn on notifications and hides Team Snapchat's chat; you can still turn notifications on in its settings. Calm mode also removes the "Open app" bar at the bottom of Facebook. Safari's own "Open in the app" banner stays when the app is installed: it only opens the app if you tap it. Turn Calm mode off for a site by picking Vanilla in the NoThis menu.
