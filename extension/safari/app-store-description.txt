The web, without the noise.

News sites and social media are built to hold on to you: bait headlines, autoplay, ads, endless feeds, and constant nudges to open the app. Web Detox clears that away, so you can read what you came for and leave when you're done.

On news sites, you get clear headlines instead of clickbait, or a calm page with just the articles. Choose how much to change for each site.

On social media, Web Detox keeps the people, groups and messages you follow, and hides ads, suggested posts, short-video feeds and trending boxes. Opening the app is always your choice.

Web Detox is for everyone. It may be especially helpful for people with ADHD, autistic people, or anyone who finds busy pages tiring or overwhelming. Less movement, fewer interruptions and fewer things competing for your attention can make the web easier to use.

You're in control: pick Vanilla to see any site as published. No tracking.
